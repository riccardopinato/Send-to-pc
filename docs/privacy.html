Send to PC / Invia al PC — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

Local transfers

Send to PC transfers files, photos, text and links directly between the Android device and a computer browser on the local network. The app does not operate a cloud backend for transferred content.

From v1.20, local transfer sessions use TLS 1.2/1.3. The phone uses a persistent local certificate and shows its SHA-256 fingerprint so the user can verify the local server identity when the browser displays a self-signed certificate warning.

Advertising and consent

The app integrates Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services may connect to Google independently of the local transfer channel. The app does not intentionally pass the contents of transferred files, text or links to AdMob.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, advertising components can process information such as IP address, product interactions, diagnostic information and device/account identifiers for purposes including advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Local history and trusted computers

Transfer history, app preferences and trusted-computer information are stored locally on the Android device. Users can clear transfer history and revoke trusted computers from the app.

Accounts

No account is required to use Send to PC.

Permissions and network access

Internet/network access is used for local device-to-browser communication and, separately, for advertising/privacy services when enabled. A foreground service is used while an active transfer session needs to remain available.

Data safety

Final Google Play Data safety declarations are based on the exact production artifact and the versions/configuration of included SDKs. This page is kept aligned with the app's documented privacy model.

Contact

Support contact and the public policy URL will be completed in the Google Play listing before Store release.
