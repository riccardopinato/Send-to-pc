## English (en)

**App name**
Send to PC

**Short description**
Transfer files, photos, text and links between Android and your PC locally.

**Full description**
Send to PC lets you move files, photos, text and links directly between your Android phone and a computer browser on the same local network.

No desktop program is required. Start a session on your phone, type the short local address in your computer browser and enter the 8-character connection code. QR code and full-link access remain available as alternatives. Verify the local certificate fingerprint when required and transfer in either direction.

Key features:
• Phone → PC and PC → phone
• Files, photos, text and links
• Local TLS-encrypted connection
• Quick Connect with short local address + connection code
• QR code + PIN and full-link fallback
• Resumable PC → phone uploads
• HTTP Range support for downloads over the encrypted session
• Pause, resume and cancel
• Multi-file batches
• Transfer progress and summaries
• Local transfer history
• Trusted computers that can be revoked
• Light, dark and system themes
• English, Italian, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese

Transferred content is not uploaded to an app-owned cloud. Advertising and privacy services use separate internet connections.

For best results, keep phone and computer on the same private local network. The computer may use Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
